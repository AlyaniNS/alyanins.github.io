description: "Airlines need to plan lounge capacity and improve booking conversion, but both require data they don't always model explicitly. Using a British Airways simulation dataset of 50,000 bookings, I built two solutions: a lounge eligibility lookup framework for Heathrow T3 capacity planning by route and departure time, and a Random Forest classifier (AUC 0.787) to predict booking completion. Key finding: purchase lead time is the strongest predictor of whether a customer completes a booking, actionable for targeted re-engagement campaigns. Findings were packaged into an executive presentation deck."
